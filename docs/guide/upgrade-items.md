# Upgrade items

Upgrade items raise a pot that is already placed to a higher tier, without breaking it. There is one for every pair of tiers, so a pot can jump straight from any tier to any higher one. The name and the texture both show the tier it upgrades **from** (the pot's band) and **to** (the arrow).

## Using an upgrade

**Sneak and use** the upgrade item on a placed pot whose tier matches the item's *from* tier. It works on a pot on its own and on a pot sitting on a campfire. One upgrade item is used.

- **Better pot to a higher tier:** nothing else changes. The pot keeps its colour, facing, lid, and every item inside it.
- **Cobblemon pot to a better tier:** the pot keeps its colour, facing and lid, but when it is on a campfire **the items inside it are dropped** on the ground.

::: tip
Upgrading a better pot is the safe way to speed up a pot that is mid-cook or wired into automation. Nothing has to be taken out first.
:::

## Obtaining

Pick an upgrade to see its recipe. Upgrades are crafted from the upgrade one tier shorter, like the pots themselves, and the ones ending at Netherite are smithing upgrades.
